# Flash campaigns

Release 1.3.0 adds seller-scoped flash campaigns. A campaign is launched from an active catalog product with a whole-percent discount, a 6, 24 or 48 hour expiry, a maximum claim quantity and one marketing channel. The server builds its audience from the newest eligible recommendation preview for each active buyer who still has consent for that exact channel. An empty audience cannot launch.

Each campaign stores an immutable product, price, audience, reason and purchase-evidence snapshot. Repeating the same seller client request returns the original campaign and never creates duplicate buyer offers. Each generated offer writes a durable delivery outbox row. The channel is an eligibility check and outbox state only: this release creates no real e-mail, postal delivery, payment, fulfillment or external inventory integration.

Campaign preview freezes the same 30-day comparison-price evidence used by personal offers. Launch copies the reference price, calculation window and catalog revision evidence into the campaign and every generated offer; a product-version change invalidates the preview instead of silently recalculating it.

The seller's configured holdout percentage is applied deterministically before launch. In pooled mode the same seller-buyer relationship remains in the control pool across campaigns; per-campaign mode includes the campaign key in assignment. Control members are frozen in `holdoutSnapshot` and receive no offer, conversation event or delivery row. Campaign reporting compares distinct treatment and control customers with an imported purchase of the same product during the campaign window. A group needs at least ten members before the UI labels the comparison as comparable; the measured difference is not presented as margin or proven causality.

Campaign quantity is a claim cap, never an independent stock pool. A buyer who accepts a campaign offer atomically creates one `CampaignReservation`; a seller/product inventory balance accepts the reservation only while its count is below the current DiscountDirect catalog stock. The campaign counter and balance update inside the same Atlas transaction, so concurrent buyers cannot claim the final unit twice. A cancellation changes each outstanding reservation to `released` exactly once and decrements that shared balance. Connector stock sync persists the latest seller-scoped `StockObservation` per provider SKU and updates the matching local product stock in the same transaction; stock reaching zero also transitions pending and accepted offers to `sold_out` with durable system evidence.

Seller APIs are `POST` and `GET /api/sellers/{sellerSlug}/campaigns/flash`, `GET /api/sellers/{sellerSlug}/campaigns/{campaignId}`, and `POST /api/sellers/{sellerSlug}/campaigns/{campaignId}/cancel`. The create body is `{productId, discountPct, quantity, expiresAt, channel, clientRequestId}`. Buyer claim remains `POST /api/offers/{offerId}/respond`; campaign offers return `CAMPAIGN_SOLD_OUT` when the shared balance or campaign window has closed.
